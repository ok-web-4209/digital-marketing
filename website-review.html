FAQ

About the review

Is the website review really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation. If the review shows your site is already doing the job, we will tell you that rather than invent problems.

How long does the review take?

We usually send it back within two to three business days.

What will I receive?

A written summary of what is working, what is losing you enquiries, and the specific changes we would make first — in order of impact. It is written for a business owner, not for a developer.

What if I do not have a website yet?

Send the form through anyway and describe your business. We will look at how your competitors are positioned locally and what it would take to compete with them.

Prefer to talk it through first?

Send us a message and describe what you are trying to fix. We will tell you whether a review, a redesign or a local SEO campaign is the right starting point.
