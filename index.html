The honest starting point

Is Your Website Costing You Potential Customers?

Most service businesses do not have a traffic problem. They have a website that loses the people who already found them.

  • The website looks outdated

    A design that looks ten years old makes an established business feel less capable than it is.

  • It is difficult to use on a phone

    Text too small to read and buttons too small to tap send people straight back to search results.

  • Visitors cannot easily request a quote

    No visible form, no click-to-call button, and a contact page hidden at the end of the menu.

  • Services are not clearly explained

    One vague page listing everything you do, instead of a real page for each service you want to sell.

  • The business does not appear in local searches

    Competitors show up for "near me" searches in your towns and you do not.

  • The website lacks service-area pages

    Nothing on the site tells Google or a homeowner which towns and counties you actually cover.

  • Good Google reviews are not shown

    Years of five-star reviews sitting on a profile page instead of building trust on your website.

  • Competitors appear more established

    Sharper photos, clearer pricing and better structure make a smaller competitor look like the safer choice.

Not sure which of these apply to you? A free review will tell you, in plain language.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a service-business website cost?

A refresh of an existing site starts at $1,000 and a new lead-generation website starts at $1,500. What moves the number is page count, how much content we write versus how much you supply, and any integrations you need. You get a written scope with a fixed figure before work starts.

How long does a website project take?

Most projects run three to six weeks from the discovery call to launch. The largest variable is content — how quickly we can get photos, service details and reviews from you. Larger sites with many service-area pages take longer, and we agree the timeline in writing up front.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, and it is a large part of what we do. We audit what you have, keep the pages and content that already earn traffic, redirect anything that has to change, and rebuild the parts costing you enquiries.

Do you provide website content?

Yes. We can write service pages, service-area pages and supporting content based on a discovery call and your existing material. If you would rather write it yourself, we will give you outlines and a word count for each page.

Is local SEO included?

The structural side is included in every website build: page titles, headings, service and service-area page structure, schema markup, internal linking and a sitemap. Ongoing local SEO — monthly content, Google Business Profile work and rank tracking — is a separate monthly service.

Can you create service-area pages?

Yes. We build a genuine page for each town or county you want to be found in, with content specific to that area. We do not generate dozens of near-identical pages with the place name swapped out, because Google treats that as thin content and it can hurt you.
