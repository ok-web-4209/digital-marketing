The four things that change a quote

Page count

Content

Whether we write your service and location pages or you supply the copy.

Integrations

FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does a service-business website cost?

A refresh of an existing site starts at $1,000 and a new lead-generation website starts at $1,500. What moves the number is page count, how much content we write versus how much you supply, and any integrations you need. You get a written scope with a fixed figure before work starts.

How long does a website project take?

Most projects run three to six weeks from the discovery call to launch. The largest variable is content — how quickly we can get photos, service details and reviews from you. Larger sites with many service-area pages take longer, and we agree the timeline in writing up front.

What is not included in the starting price?

Domain registration, hosting and any paid third-party tools are billed to you directly at cost — we do not mark them up or resell them. Photography, logo design and paid advertising budgets are separate. Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted before it is started.

Do you require a long contract for local SEO?

No. Local SEO runs month to month. We ask for a realistic commitment of three to six months because that is how long meaningful movement takes, but you are not locked in.

Is local SEO included?

The structural side is included in every website build: page titles, headings, service and service-area page structure, schema markup, internal linking and a sitemap. Ongoing local SEO — monthly content, Google Business Profile work and rank tracking — is a separate monthly service.

Will I own my website?

Yes. You own the website, the domain, the content and the analytics accounts. Everything is registered in your name and there is nothing to unlock if you ever decide to work with someone else.
