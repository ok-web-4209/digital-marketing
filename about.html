About

A small studio doing one thing properly

Design Rank Studio builds websites and runs local SEO for service businesses. That is the whole offering. We are not a full-service agency and we are not trying to become one.

Request a Free Website ReviewSee our work

How we work

Remote, direct and specific

Design Rank Studio works remotely with service businesses across the United States. Everything happens over calls, email and messages — there are no in-person visits, and none are needed to build a website or run a local search campaign.

You deal directly with the person doing the work. There is no account manager passing messages along and no junior handed the project after the sale. When you ask why a page is structured the way it is, you get the actual reason.

We start with strategy: what you sell, where you sell it, which jobs are worth the most, and what your competitors are already doing well. Design follows from that. So does the local SEO structure, which is built in from the start rather than added later.

We would rather tell you a project is not worth doing than sell you one. If a review shows your site is already working, we will say so.

What we do

Lead-generation website design, website redesign, local SEO and landing pages for US service businesses.

Who we do it for

Primarily specialty home-service companies — tree services, concrete, fencing, landscaping, pool, garage doors, roofing and outdoor services — plus real estate firms, solo attorneys and other established local businesses.

What we do not do

Guarantee rankings or lead volume. Lock you into contracts you cannot leave. Hold your domain, hosting or analytics hostage. Sell you services you do not need.

Why work with us

A studio that understands how service businesses actually win work

Strategy before design

We start with your services, your service area and the jobs you actually want more of. Layout decisions come after that, not before.

Built to generate enquiries

Every page has an obvious next step: call, request a quote or send a message. Nothing important is buried two clicks deep.

Local SEO from the beginning

Service pages, service-area pages and page titles are structured for local search while the site is being built, not bolted on later.

Clear pricing and project scope

You get a written scope with page count, deliverables and timeline before work starts, so there are no surprise invoices.

Mobile-first development

Most service-business visitors arrive on a phone. Layouts are designed for that screen first, then expanded for desktop.

Direct communication

You talk to the person doing the work. No account manager relaying messages, no ticket queue.

No unnecessary technical complexity

Fast, maintainable websites without plugin sprawl or systems you will need to pay someone else to operate.

Designed for long-term growth

Structure that lets you add services, locations and landing pages over time without rebuilding the site.

How projects run

A clear process, from first call to launch and beyond

You always know what stage the project is at and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We go through your services, the areas you cover, the jobs worth the most to you, your competitors and what is working now.

    • Services and job types
    • Target towns and counties
    • Current site and analytics review
    • Competitor comparison
  2. 02

    Strategy and Content

    We map the page structure, the conversion path and the local search opportunities, then write or shape the content to match.

    • Sitemap and page plan
    • Keyword and service-area research
    • Conversion flow and calls to action
    • Content outlines or full copy
  3. 03

    Design and Development

    We design and build the site mobile-first, with quote forms, click-to-call, review displays and clean, fast code.

    • Mobile-first design
    • Quote forms and click-to-call
    • Reviews and trust elements
    • Speed and accessibility checks
  4. 04

    Launch and Growth

    We test everything, launch, connect analytics, and give you the option to keep improving rankings and conversions.

    • Pre-launch testing
    • Analytics and call tracking setup
    • Search Console and sitemap submission
    • Optional ongoing local SEO
